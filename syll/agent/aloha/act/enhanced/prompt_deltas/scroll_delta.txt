## Scroll Action Guidance

Scrolling should be done in **small, deliberate increments**:
- Specify direction (up/down) and approximate distance.
- After scrolling, re-observe the screen to verify the target content is visible.

Common pitfalls:
- Overshooting: scrolling too far past the target.
- Undershooting: not scrolling enough to reveal the target.
- Dynamic content: infinite scroll pages may keep loading.

Strategy:
1. Scroll a small amount (2-3 "clicks").
2. Take a new observation.
3. If the target is not yet visible, scroll again.
4. If the target was missed, scroll back in the opposite direction.
